Title                   : SIGIL Vanilla DOOM (DOS) Editon
Filename                : SIGILDOS.wad
Release date            : v1.0: 2022/02/24
Version                 : 1.0
Author of original maps	: John Romero
Modifications			: the_kovic
Email Address           : [redacted]

Description             : The original release of SIGIL by John Romero
requires a limit removing sourceport. This modification of SIGIL v1.21
simplifies level geometry to adhere to the limits of the DOOM.EXE
version 1.9 (most notably the visplane and drawseg limits). Changes were
made with the intention to keep the original level design and visuals
as intact as possible. Despite that, there *are* changes and demos desync
on the new maps.
