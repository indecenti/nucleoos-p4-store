Tic Tac Toe

Cos'è

Tris contro un amico o contro un bot minimax imbattibile, con tre livelli. Tocca per giocare.

Comandi

tocchi e trascinamenti valgono come mouse e frecce. Funzionano anche tastiera o joypad USB/Bluetooth (joypad: croce = frecce, A = Spazio, B = Ctrl, Start = Invio). Il gesto Indietro è Esc; due volte di fila chiude il gioco.

Tic Tac Toe

What it is

Noughts and crosses against a friend or an unbeatable minimax bot with three levels. Tap to play.

Controls

taps and swipes work as the mouse and arrow keys. A USB or Bluetooth keyboard or gamepad works too (pad: d-pad = arrows, A = Space, B = Ctrl, Start = Enter). The back gesture is Escape; twice in a row closes the game.

Credits and license

Tic Tac Toe by Rafael Jacov Medel, MIT (https://github.com/rafaeljacov/tictactoe_minimax, commit c16b90b84412). A LÖVE game running on the NucleoOS Lua App engine; the port adds only nucleo.lua (on-screen controls) and the changes listed in ports/luaapp/LOVE_GAMES.md. Fonts and music of the original are not included.
