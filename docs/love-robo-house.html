Robo House

Cos'è

Un platform a schermata singola: corri, sali le scale e salta i robot per salvare i gattini della casa. Croce direzionale e Salto sullo schermo.

Comandi

I tasti sullo schermo sono disegnati sopra il gioco; tocchi e trascinamenti valgono come mouse e frecce. Funzionano anche tastiera o joypad USB/Bluetooth (joypad: croce = frecce, A = Spazio, B = Ctrl, Start = Invio). Il gesto Indietro è Esc; due volte di fila chiude il gioco.

Robo House

version 1.0.0 · License: GPL-3.0 · Project site

Robo House

What it is

A one-screen platformer: run, climb the ladders and jump over the robots to rescue the kittens in the house. On-screen d-pad and Jump.

Controls

On-screen buttons are drawn over the game; taps and swipes work as the mouse and arrow keys. A USB or Bluetooth keyboard or gamepad works too (pad: d-pad = arrows, A = Space, B = Ctrl, Start = Enter). The back gesture is Escape; twice in a row closes the game.

Credits and license

Robo House by Foppygames, GPL-3.0 (https://github.com/Foppygames/robo-house, commit a84da6333222). A LÖVE game running on the NucleoOS Lua App engine; the port adds only nucleo.lua (on-screen controls) and the changes listed in ports/luaapp/LOVE_GAMES.md. Fonts and music of the original are not included.
