Slide Puzzle

Cos'è

Il gioco del quindici: rimetti in ordine le tessere numerate. Tocca una tessera vicina al buco per farla scorrere, oppure scorri.

Comandi

tocchi e trascinamenti valgono come mouse e frecce. Funzionano anche tastiera o joypad USB/Bluetooth (joypad: croce = frecce, A = Spazio, B = Ctrl, Start = Invio). Il gesto Indietro è Esc; due volte di fila chiude il gioco.

Slide Puzzle

What it is

The fifteen puzzle: put the numbered tiles back in order. Tap a tile next to the gap to slide it, or swipe.

Controls

taps and swipes work as the mouse and arrow keys. A USB or Bluetooth keyboard or gamepad works too (pad: d-pad = arrows, A = Space, B = Ctrl, Start = Enter). The back gesture is Escape; twice in a row closes the game.

Credits and license

Slide Puzzle by Przemysław Bądaruk (Przemekkkth), after Al Sweigart, MIT (https://github.com/Przemekkkth/love-slide-puzzle, commit 1e2b893f897b). A LÖVE game running on the NucleoOS Lua App engine; the port adds only nucleo.lua (on-screen controls) and the changes listed in ports/luaapp/LOVE_GAMES.md. Fonts and music of the original are not included.
