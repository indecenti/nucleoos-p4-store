2048

Cos'è

Fai scorrere le tessere e unisci i numeri uguali fino a 2048. Scorri in qualsiasi direzione; tastiera: frecce.

Comandi

tocchi e trascinamenti valgono come mouse e frecce. Funzionano anche tastiera o joypad USB/Bluetooth (joypad: croce = frecce, A = Spazio, B = Ctrl, Start = Invio). Il gesto Indietro è Esc; due volte di fila chiude il gioco.

2048

Slide the tiles and merge equal numbers until you reach 2048. Swipe in any direction; keyboard: arrows. A LÖVE game on the Lua App engine.

version 1.0.0 · License: MIT · Project site

2048

What it is

Slide the tiles and merge equal numbers until you reach 2048. Swipe in any direction; keyboard: arrows.

Controls

taps and swipes work as the mouse and arrow keys. A USB or Bluetooth keyboard or gamepad works too (pad: d-pad = arrows, A = Space, B = Ctrl, Start = Enter). The back gesture is Escape; twice in a row closes the game.

Credits and license

2048 by Przemek Bądaruk (Przemekkkth), MIT (https://github.com/Przemekkkth/love-2048, commit bb7fe3a14728). A LÖVE game running on the NucleoOS Lua App engine; the port adds only nucleo.lua (on-screen controls) and the changes listed in ports/luaapp/LOVE_GAMES.md. Fonts and music of the original are not included.
