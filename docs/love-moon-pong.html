Moon Pong

Cos'è

Pong contro il computer. Tieni premute le frecce sullo schermo (o Su/Giù su tastiera o joypad) per muovere la racchetta.

Comandi

I tasti sullo schermo sono disegnati sopra il gioco; tocchi e trascinamenti valgono come mouse e frecce. Funzionano anche tastiera o joypad USB/Bluetooth (joypad: croce = frecce, A = Spazio, B = Ctrl, Start = Invio). Il gesto Indietro è Esc; due volte di fila chiude il gioco.

Moon Pong

What it is

Pong against the computer. Hold the on-screen arrows (or Up/Down on a keyboard or pad) to move your paddle.

Controls

On-screen buttons are drawn over the game; taps and swipes work as the mouse and arrow keys. A USB or Bluetooth keyboard or gamepad works too (pad: d-pad = arrows, A = Space, B = Ctrl, Start = Enter). The back gesture is Escape; twice in a row closes the game.

Credits and license

Moon Pong by Om Rawaley, Apache-2.0 (https://github.com/omrawaley/Moon-Pong, commit ea35ba83cb68). A LÖVE game running on the NucleoOS Lua App engine; the port adds only nucleo.lua (on-screen controls) and the changes listed in ports/luaapp/LOVE_GAMES.md. Fonts and music of the original are not included.
